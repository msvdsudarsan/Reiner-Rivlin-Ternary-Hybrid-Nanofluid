# Reference verification

`data/reference_audit_ledger.csv` lists every work cited in the manuscript or in
its supplementary material (52 entries: 41 in the main bibliography and 11 cited
only in the supplement), with the level of checking reached for each.

Three levels are kept apart:

1. **Metadata**: authors, title, journal, volume, pages and DOI compared with a
   publisher or index record.
2. **Abstract-level support**: the publisher abstract supports the specific
   statement for which the work is cited.
3. **Full-text support**: the full text was read for that statement.

Most entries reach levels 1 and 2. Full-text support is recorded only where the
ledger says so; foundational works (von Karman 1921, Cochran 1934, Reiner 1945,
Rivlin 1948) and the Baker-Ericksen and Ericksen (1955) notes are bibliographic
records. The Ericksen (1957) comparison is for constant coefficients only. The
dense-suspension works (Couturier et al. 2011, Boyer et al. 2011, Dai et al.
2013) were checked at abstract level. A DOI that resolves is not treated as
evidence that a work supports a claim.

Di Landro et al. (2003) was read in full (PDF supplied by the corresponding author). Only its tabulated Psi_1 and |Psi_2| values and the plateau of its viscosity figure (read from the figure by pixel measurement, about 10 percent) are used.
